TikTok video downloader for Android and iPhone

You do not need a downloader app. The tool works in the browser on Android and iOS, so you can save a public TikTok clip as MP4, MP3, or a story in a few taps.

Quick answer

Copy the TikTok link in the app, open this site in your phone's browser, paste the link, choose MP4, MP3, or Story, close the ad, and the file goes to your Downloads folder.

Step-by-step on a phone

  1. Copy the link

    Open the TikTok clip, tap Share, then Copy link.

  2. Paste and choose

    Switch to this browser tab, paste the link, and pick the format you want.

  3. Close the ad and find the file

    After the short ad, the save starts. On iPhone, open Files → Downloads. On Android, open the Downloads app or your Downloads folder.

Browser tips

  • iPhone: Safari saves to Files → Downloads. Make sure Downloads is enabled in Safari settings.
  • Android: Chrome saves to the Downloads folder shown in your file manager.
  • Slow data: pick a lower resolution for a smaller file and faster save.
  • Private mode: downloads may be blocked; use a normal tab.

Why use the browser instead of an app?

A browser tool needs no installation, no login, and no storage permission. You paste a link and get the file. That also means fewer updates to worry about and a smaller attack surface than a random third-party app.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app on Android or iPhone?

No. Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge on your phone is enough. The tool runs in the browser.

Where does the file go on my phone?

It saves to the browser's default Downloads folder. On iPhone, open Files and look in Downloads; on Android, check the Downloads app or folder.

Can I save large videos on mobile data?

Yes, but pick a lower resolution if your connection is slow. The file is prepared the same way regardless of size.

Does it work on both Android and iOS?

Yes. Any modern mobile browser with JavaScript support can use the downloader.
