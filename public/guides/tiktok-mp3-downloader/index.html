TikTok MP3 downloader

Want just the sound from a TikTok clip? Switch the tool to MP3 mode and save a clean 192 kbps audio file directly to your device — no screen recording, no extra software.

Convert a TikTok to MP3 →

Quick answer

Paste a public TikTok link, select MP3 under Format, click Download, choose Download MP3, then close the ad. The audio file starts saving right away.

How to extract TikTok audio

  1. Copy the video link

    Open the clip in TikTok, tap Share, then Copy link.

  2. Switch to MP3 mode

    Paste the link, then select the MP3 format button before you click Download.

  3. Save the audio file

    Choose Download MP3 from the result. The audio is prepared while the ad is open; closing it starts the save.

Why 192 kbps MP3?

192 kbps keeps spoken words crisp and music acceptably detailed while staying small enough for quick sharing. Because the audio comes from the original stream, you avoid the extra quality loss of recording your speaker output.

When MP3 mode will not work

  • Private, deleted, or age-restricted clips.
  • Image-only slideshows with no audio track.
  • Live streams or links that no longer resolve.
  • Direct story media without sound.

Frequently asked questions

What bitrate is the MP3?

Audio is encoded at 192 kbps, a good balance between clarity and file size for speech and music clips.

Can I convert a TikTok slideshow to MP3?

Only clips with an audio track can be converted. Image-only slideshows may have no sound to extract.

Is the video included in the MP3?

No. MP3 mode extracts only the sound. Choose MP4 mode if you want the picture too.

Do I need to upload a file?

No. Paste the public link and the audio is prepared from the source stream. Nothing is uploaded from your device.
